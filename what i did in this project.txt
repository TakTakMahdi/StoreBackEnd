IUnitOfWork.cs file 
. Example of why it matters: creating a Commande involves inserting the Commande row, several LigneCommande rows, and decrementing Produit.Stock — four separate writes that must all succeed or all fail together. Without Unit of Work, each repository might call SaveChanges() separately, risking a half-committed state if something fails midway. With it, the service calls unitOfWork.Commandes.AddAsync(...), then unitOfWork.Produits.UpdateAsync(...), then one unitOfWork.SaveChangesAsync() at the end.
